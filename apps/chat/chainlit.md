# Seeker

Ask about the channel's live streams. Answers come only from the processed videos, say who said what, and link to
the video at the time it is said.

- **Strategy:** pick it at the top (how the transcripts were processed + how passages are found). The description
  shows its evaluation score.
- **Your Gemini key:** asked once in this browser (create one at https://aistudio.google.com/apikey). It stays in
  this browser and is not saved on the server.
- **Model:** in the settings. Each Gemini model has its own free daily quota; switch when one is used up.
